About me

Dietitian. Athlete. Curious human.

A little about me · one

The Human

I've been running for over 10 years and I guess you could say I'm pretty good at running through life too. I'm married, live in Lebanon, love to travel, and between work, family, running, and everything in between, I'm always on the run.

I'm happiest when I'm moving, cooking something colorful, travelling somewhere new, or helping women feel stronger, healthier, and happier in their own skin.

I'm also endlessly curious about health and wellbeing, which has taken me beyond nutrition and into things like Chinese medicine and holistic approaches to health. I love learning about different ways of looking at the body, health, and wellbeing.

And while health and nutrition are a huge part of who I am, they're not my only passions. In another life, I'd probably be a fashion designer. I've always loved fashion, styling, and the way clothes can be a form of creativity and self-expression.

A little about me · two

The Professional Me

I'm a licensed dietitian, certified sports nutritionist, public health professional, and former track & field athlete. I believe feeling your best comes from finding the right balance between how we eat, how we move, and how we live.

My journey started on the track. After more than 10 years competing in the 400m and 400m hurdles, I learned firsthand that nutrition, recovery, mindset, and consistency matter just as much as training. That experience still shapes the way I approach health today.

I studied Nutrition & Dietetics at Lebanese American University and later completed a Master's in Public Health at the American University of Beirut. I've spent 8+ years working across public health, health and nutrition, research, and humanitarian programmes with organizations including UNICEF, WFP, AUB, and the Ministry of Public Health. I'm also a certified sports nutritionist and have explored Traditional Chinese Medicine through studies in Shanghai, China.

Today, I work across public health, nutrition consulting, and individual care, with a particular passion for helping people, especially women, feel stronger, healthier, and more confident in their bodies.

SheOnTheRun and DietOnTheRun are where all these worlds come together: sharing what I've learned, what the research says, and what actually works in real life.

Because being your best isn't about doing everything perfectly. It's about feeling good enough to go after the life you want.

My journey

2014 → 2026

From the track, to the clinic, to the field — and back again.

2014

It Started With Running

I started training in track & field, beginning with the 800m and 1500m before finding my way to the 400m. I always had a soft spot for the 400m hurdles and eventually competed in it, earning 2nd place nationally.

2016

Nutrition Meets Sport

I completed my academic coursework in Nutrition & Dietetics at LAU and joined the American Coordinated Program, completing my 9-month dietetic internship at LAU Medical Center–Rizk Hospital.

2017

Becoming a Dietitian

I graduated and became a licensed dietitian, then began my Master's in Public Health at AUB, specializing in Epidemiology and Biostatistics.

2018

From Nutrition to Public Health

I joined UNICEF as one of Lebanon's first Youth UN Volunteers, as part of a global pilot program focused on child survival. I was one of just six young people selected in Lebanon — all while completing my Master's degree.

2019

Entering the UN World

I graduated from AUB with my Master's in Public Health and joined the World Food Programme (WFP) Lebanon as a Monitoring & Evaluation Associate. Over the next 2.5 years, I took on different roles and grew deeper into the world of public health.

2022

Consulting & New Perspectives

I began consulting with UNICEF as an Information Management Consultant, working at the intersection of health, data, and public health.

2023

Back to Lebanon's Health System

I began consulting with the Ministry of Public Health through AUB, continuing to work in public health while bringing together my background in nutrition, health, and data.

2024

Back on Track

After stepping away from professional training during COVID, I returned to track & field and committed to a full year of focused training for the 400m. I went deeper into sports nutrition and became a certified sports nutritionist. And somewhere along the way, I decided it was time to bring all these pieces together — I started SheOnTheRun, a women-only space built around running, movement and community, and dedicated more time to my nutrition practice, DietOnTheRun.

2025

A New Way of Looking at Health

I travelled to China for a month to study Traditional Chinese Medicine, following my curiosity about different approaches to health and wellbeing. Back home, SheOnTheRun continued to grow through runs, events, and a community of women moving together, while DietOnTheRun grew through my work with women one-on-one.

2026

Bringing It All Together

Today, I'm bringing together everything I've learned along the way: the athlete, the dietitian, the sports nutritionist, the public health professional, and the curious human who never stops learning. Through SheOnTheRun and DietOnTheRun, I'm building a space where women can learn, move, fuel themselves well, and feel good doing it. And this is only the beginning.

01 — Who

A dietitian who came to nutrition through sport, and to public health through wanting it to reach more than one room at a time.

Ten years on the track. A licence, a Master's, eight years of fieldwork. A month in Shanghai studying Traditional Chinese Medicine, because I was curious.

All of it points one way: helping women feel stronger, healthier and more at home in their own bodies.

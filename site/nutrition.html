My approach

Personalised, evidence-based, realistic

I don't believe in one-size-fits-all diets or quick fixes. We look at your lifestyle, goals, preferences, routines, relationship with food, and movement to create an approach that actually fits your life.

Whatever brings you here, we start where you are — not where you think you should be. Because good nutrition isn't about eating perfectly. It's about finding a way of eating that helps you feel good, stay strong, and live the life you want.

Questions

Before you book

Do I need to know which package is right for me?

No. Book the free 15-minute discovery call and we'll work out the right starting point together — including whether a single consultation is all you need.

Can I work with you if I'm not in Beirut?

Yes. Every consultation and package is available online by video call, wherever you are. The only element that requires an in-person visit is body composition testing.

Do you give meal plans?

Where a plan is genuinely useful, yes — a personalised one built around your routine, preferences and goals. But a plan on its own rarely changes anything, so the coaching around it matters just as much.

What if I have a medical condition?

Bring your relevant health history to your initial consultation and we'll build your plan around it. For conditions requiring medical management, I work alongside your doctor rather than replacing their advice.

How is a package different from booking single sessions?

A package bundles your initial consultation with follow-ups and between-session support at a lower combined price. Real change comes from the follow-ups, not the first appointment.

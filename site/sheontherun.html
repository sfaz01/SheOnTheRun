The story

Why I started SheOnTheRun

SheOnTheRun started with a simple question I kept hearing from women: "How do I start running?"

At the time, there weren't really running communities in Lebanon for women who simply wanted to run for fun. Most running clubs were focused on training, performance, and competition. So I decided to create the space I wished existed.

A space where women could show up, run at their own pace, take it easy, have fun, and feel safe — without pressure, competition, or judgment.

And then something funny happened: running took off. What started as a small idea grew alongside a much bigger national and global running movement. Suddenly, running clubs were everywhere.

But SheOnTheRun had already become something bigger than running.

Today, it has grown into a women-only active and wellness community where movement is the starting point for connection, confidence, and growth. Alongside our runs, we bring women together through yoga, Pilates, creative workshops, art, retreats, and other experiences designed to help us move, connect, and make space for ourselves.

Because SheOnTheRun was never really just about running. It's about creating a space where women feel comfortable showing up exactly as they are — and leave a little stronger, a little happier, and a little more connected.
